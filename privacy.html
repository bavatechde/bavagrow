Privacy Policy for the BavaGrow app

Last updated: 27 October 2026. The German version is legally binding.

In short

  • BavaGrow needs no account and no sign-in.
  • Your plants, logs, photos and settings are stored only on your device. I have no access to them.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Data only leaves your device if you connect Home Assistant, share a backup or buy BavaGrow Pro.

1. Controller

Justin Plaschka, [STREET AND NUMBER], [POSTCODE CITY], Germany
Email: [E-MAIL]

2. Data on your device

Everything you enter in BavaGrow – plants, watering and feeding logs, notes, measurements, reminders and settings – is stored locally on your device only. It is not sent to me or to third parties and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

If you have enabled Android backup to your Google account, Android may back up the app data there. This is controlled by Android/Google, not by BavaGrow; Home Assistant credentials are excluded.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: only when you take a photo of a plant.
  • Choosing photos: via the Android photo picker. The app only sees the pictures you pick and stores a copy inside the app.
  • Saving pictures: only when you explicitly save a photo to your gallery.
  • Notifications: for reminders you create. They are scheduled on the device, not sent from a server.
  • Internet: only for Home Assistant and the Pro purchase (see below).

4. Home Assistant (optional)

If you connect BavaGrow to your Home Assistant server, the app fetches readings (e.g. temperature and humidity) directly from the address you enter – on your home network or via remote access such as Nabu Casa. The connection runs directly between your device and your server; I receive no data. Your access token is stored encrypted on the device.

5. Backups

When you create a backup, the app creates a file and opens the Android share menu. You alone decide where the file goes. The backup may include your Home Assistant token, so keep it safe.

6. Buying BavaGrow Pro

BavaGrow Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled by Google Play; I do not receive your payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

To verify the purchase and restore it on your devices, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., 1032 E Brandon Blvd #3003, Brandon, FL 33511, USA). It processes a random anonymous app ID, the Google Play purchase receipt (product, time, price, country), technical data such as app and Android version, and your IP address. Your name, email address and app content are not transmitted.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the purchase contract). RevenueCat processes data in the USA; the transfer is safeguarded by EU Standard Contractual Clauses. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

7. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). No cookies, no tracking.

8. Children

BavaGrow is not directed at children under 16.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since I receive no app data, you can change or delete most data directly in the app. For questions about Pro purchases, email me at the address above.

10. Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects what data is processed, I will update this policy. The version published here applies.
